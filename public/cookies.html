Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

What we use

Satsuma only uses cookies that are strictly necessary to run the application. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Essential cookies

Session cookie. Keeps you signed in and remembers your current organization between requests.

CSRF token. Protects forms and API requests against cross-site request forgery.

Remember-me cookie. Set only if you choose "remember me" at sign-in, so your session survives a browser restart.

Third-party cookies

We do not embed third-party scripts that set cookies on the dashboard.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Because our cookies are required for sign-in and request security, the service will not work if you disable them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@satsuma.app.
