Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Overview

Satsuma is a service for managing WordPress sites. This policy describes what information we collect when you use the service, why we collect it, and how it is handled.

Information we collect

Account information. When you register we store your name, email address and a hashed password, plus the organizations you belong to and their members.

Site data. For each site you connect, the Satsuma agent reports technical inventory: the site URL, WordPress and PHP versions, and the installed plugins and themes with their versions. Scans only run when you request them or on a schedule you configure.

Usage data. We store operational data needed to run the service — connection status, scan timestamps, credit usage and notification history.

How we use it

Collected data is used solely to provide the service: displaying your sites and their status, alerting you to updates and vulnerabilities, and delivering notifications you have enabled. We do not sell your data.

Third-party services

Email notifications are delivered through our email provider (Resend). Vulnerability lookups use the Wordfence Intelligence feed, which receives software identifiers — not your site URLs or personal data. Performance audits are performed by Google PageSpeed Insights, which receives the URL of the page being audited.

Retention and deletion

Site inventory and notification data is retained while your account is active. Removing a site deletes its stored inventory; deleting your account removes your personal data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@satsuma.app.
